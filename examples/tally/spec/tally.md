# Tally

A counter that adds amounts up to a limit.

## Counting

- The total equals the sum of the supplied amounts. {{tally}}, {{total}} · {#total-holds}
- One step adds at most the limit, so the tally never grows past it in one add. {{add}}, {{limits}} · {#one-step-is-capped}
- The average is the total over the count of amounts. {{average}}, {{total}} · {#average-of-amounts}
